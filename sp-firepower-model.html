Net Debt uses cash with the haircut you set (default 25%, in line with S&P surplus-cash treatment for IG corporates).

Methodology. S&P corporate methodology (Nov 2013, updated): Anchor = Business Risk Profile × Financial Risk Profile. The FRP is set off two core ratios — FFO/Debt and Debt/EBITDA — under the Standard volatility table: Minimal (>60% / <1.5x), Modest (45–60% / 1.5–2x), Intermediate (30–45% / 2–3x), Significant (20–30% / 3–4x), Aggressive (12–20% / 4–5x), Highly Leveraged (<12% / >5x). Supplementary ratios (FFO IntCov, Debt/Cap) cross-check. This model assumes BRP = Satisfactory and uses the FRP→rating mapping for that BRP. Adjust BRP in code if your business profile differs.
