Methodology. Pro-forma metrics use S&P corporate methodology under the Standard volatility table — Financial Risk Profile (FRP) read off FFO/Debt and Net Debt/EBITDA, mapped to a rating assuming Business Risk Profile = Satisfactory. FFO ≈ EBITDA − cash interest − cash tax (pre-WC). Funding waterfall allocates the year-by-year cash need across cash above operational floor, RCF 1, RCF 2, SCF and new term debt per the selected scenario rule. Incremental interest from new debt and RCF draws is reflected from the year of action. Divestment proceeds reduce gross debt before applying the waterfall.   Disclaimer. Internal indicative model.
