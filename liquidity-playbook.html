Methodology. Each cell shows pro-forma Adj. Net Debt = base Net Debt + litigation outflow + net transaction value (top), and the resulting ratio against the keyed-in EBITDA, FFO or FOCF (bottom). Net transaction value is signed: positive = acquisition consideration, negative = divestment proceeds; the two can be netted within the same period. Cells are coloured against the S&P scorecard (standard volatility table). Liquidity total = period-end Cash + undrawn committed and uncommitted facilities. Negative net debt (net cash) is shown mechanically; ratios in that quadrant are informational.
