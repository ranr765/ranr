Monsoon-season notes · July & August

  • Sea caution: at Varkala & Kovalam the sea is rough in monsoon — go for the cliffs, cafés and sunsets, not swimming.
  • Best season for Ayurveda: Karkidakam (mid-July–mid-August) is traditionally the ideal month for treatments.
  • Hills: Munnar & Wayanad are at their greenest; check road conditions after heavy rain, carry leech socks for trails.
  • Waterfalls (Athirappilly especially) are at full thunder right now.
  • Backwaters: monsoon means fewer crowds and better houseboat rates. Nehru Trophy boat race is the 2nd Saturday of August.
  • Pack a compact umbrella, quick-dry clothes and mosquito repellent.
